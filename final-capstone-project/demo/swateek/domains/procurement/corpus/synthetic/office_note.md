# Office note on procurement practice

**SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT.** This note was written for the DLlamas capstone. It doesn't describe a real office.

1. This office is a central ministry/department office. For procurement it follows only the frozen public instruments indexed with this note:
   - General Financial Rules, 2017 (consolidated up to 31 January 2026)
   - the Department of Expenditure manuals for goods (2024), non-consultancy services (2025), consultancy services (2025), and works (2025)
   - the Delegation of Financial Powers Rules, 2024
   - GeM General Terms and Conditions 4.0 (v1.27)
   - the GeM Incident Management policy
   - the Public Procurement (Preference to Make in India) Order as revised on 19 July 2024

2. This note doesn't add, change, or relax any rule in those instruments.

3. Each bid case is evaluated against the evaluation sheet stored with that case: its mandatory conditions, scored criteria, and weights. No other sheet is used.

4. Any evaluation the assistant produces is a draft for the evaluation committee. Award, purchase orders, and debarment are decided by the competent authority, not by the assistant.
