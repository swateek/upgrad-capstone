**Note** – This is for informational purposes only - For beer, wine, and distilled spirits, some reduced tax rates and tax credits are only available if you meet criteria related to production, processing, or foreign assignments. Reduced tax rates and tax credits may also be limited if you are part of a controlled group or a Single Taxpayer arrangement. See the [CBMA page](/regulated-commodities/beverage-alcohol/cbma/craft-beverage-modernization-and-tax-reform-cbmtra) for more information. For distilled spirits, your effective tax rate may be lower based on tax credits for eligible wine or eligible flavor content. See [TTB Industry Circular 2018-4](/public-information/industry-circulars/ttb-industry-circulars-18-4).
